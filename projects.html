Abstract

The ROFIRFUTURE project explores the adaptation of Romanian provenances of silver fir (Abies alba) to major abiotic stress and the content of chemical compounds in the bark. The goal is to identify genetic resources with adequate response to salt, water, and cold stress, with applications for afforestation and forest restoration. The bark of silver fir is analyzed by FT-IR and HPLC to discover genotypes with valuable potential in industry, pharmaceuticals, and cosmetics.

Project Objective

Evaluation of different Romanian provenances of silver fir to identify forest genetic resources resistant or tolerant to abiotic stress and assessment of the chemical composition of bark for industrially and medicinally useful components.

  • Project Duration: 6 months

About the Project

The TECH-BRAD project is implemented within the Innovation Voucher (CEC) competition - PNCDI IV, aiming to develop and transfer modern technology to the beneficiary for stimulating silver fir seed germination capacity and producing forest seedlings in containerized system.

The project addresses the need for efficient technologies in forest reproduction material production, considering the growing demand for quality forest seedlings, intensification of afforestation programs, and forest adaptation to climate change.

Through implementation of research results, the project aims to increase seedling production efficiency, reduce technological costs, and obtain superior quality planting material.

Project Objectives

  • Evaluate quality of seed material used
  • Identify the most efficient methods to stimulate silver fir seed germination capacity
  • Comparative testing of different substrates for containerized seedling production
  • Experimental validation of proposed technology
  • Technology transfer to beneficiary and implementation in economic activities

Project Activities

  1. Activity 1: Seed preparation and application of germination stimulation treatments
  2. Activity 2: Germination monitoring and comparative evaluation of applied treatments
  3. Activity 3: Seedling production in containerized system using different substrate variants and evaluation of growth parameters
  4. Activity 4: Technology transfer to beneficiary and development of technological guide for implementation

Expected Results

  • Identification of optimal treatment for stimulating silver fir seed germination
  • Optimization of containerized seedling production technology
  • Reduction of seedling material losses
  • Shortened production cycle
  • Development of technological guide for immediate implementation in beneficiary activities
  • Increased company competitiveness through economic activity diversification
